3D Commerce

Eliminating Studio Friction: The Pivot to Virtual Product Assets

Result: 67% cost reduction. Zero studio travel. Infinite SKU variations.

A custom brand was locked in an expensive cycle of physical photoshoots. Every minor product change required shipping hardware states away to a studio. We implemented a PBR asset pipeline and WebGL configurator, allowing for infinite variations and instant updates. Reusable 3D assets now form their core marketing engine, providing unmeasurable long-term value.

RevOps

The $500K Conversation: Whale Curve Analysis Exposes 40 Underwater Dealers

Result: $430K recovered annually by enforcing minimum order thresholds.

A national building products manufacturer needed to prove which dealers were truly profitable. We ran an Activity-Based Costing workshop and plotted the Whale Curve, exposing 40 accounts where cost-to-serve exceeded revenue. We delivered a scorecard to enforce minimum thresholds and recovered nearly a half-million in annual margin.

MarTech

Bridging the Configuration Gap: Connecting Design to the Sales Pipeline

Result: Closed the "Configurator-to-Cart" loop. Real-time lead visibility.

A retailer allowed customers to "build their product" online, but the data hit a dead end—the pipeline stopped at the configurator. We rebuilt the backend to capture these custom builds as actionable leads, injecting them directly into the CRM with full spec sheets, turning a "toy" into a high-intent conversion engine.
