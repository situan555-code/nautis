The 2026 Integration Standard: 4 Key Sync Points

1. Bi-Directional Inventory Sync

"Out of Stock" is a better customer experience than "Order Cancelled." Your ERP must feed your storefront every 60 seconds. Companies with real-time sync see a 14% lift in repeat customer rates.

2. Contract & Tiered Pricing Logic

In B2B, one price does not fit all. Your integration must respect complex pricing tiers, volume discounts, and individual contracts. When a "Gold Tier" customer logs in, they should see *their* price. This alone increases average order value by 22%.

3. Automated Order & Shipping Lifecycle

The moment an order is placed on the site, it should exist in the ERP. The moment a tracking number is generated, it should be emailed to the customer. This "Zero-Touch" flow reduces customer service inquiries by 45%.

4. Credit Limits & Terms Management

Integrated systems let B2B buyers check their outstanding balance, pay invoices via ACH, and place orders against their credit limits directly on the web. This self-service portal is the #1 requested feature from B2B buyers in 2026.
