The 2026 Complexity Trap: Why Spreadsheets are Killing Your Growth

Modern commerce is too complex for manual data entry. Here’s why:

  • Channel-Specific Rules — Amazon needs different bullet points than your own site. Walmart needs different image sizes than Wayfair.
  • The "Shadow Catalog" — Different versions of product data floating around in different departments (Sales, Marketing, Web).
  • The Update Lag — It takes 2 weeks to update a price across all channels, during which you're either losing margin or losing sales.
  • Rich Media Demands — Modern buyers want 360-degree views, 3D renders, and technical videos. Managing these in a folder structure is impossible at scale.

For a company with 5,000+ SKUs, these "small" data frictions often result in $300k+ in lost annual revenue from errors and delays.
