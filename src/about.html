About

Small by design.
Close to the work.

Sunder & Co. is an Ohio-based creative and systems partner for businesses that need a stronger customer-facing presence.

Origin

Why this exists.

A strong business can still look unclear from the outside. The website, search presence, visuals, messaging, and follow-up may no longer reflect the quality of the work behind them.

Sunder & Co. brings those customer-facing pieces into one focused conversation, then improves the parts that can make the biggest visible difference first.

Based in Ohio with eastern Ohio roots, Sunder & Co. works locally and remotely with businesses across the United States.

Philosophy

What we believe.

"Make the outside match the work inside."

The goal is a clearer, more credible presence that reflects the real quality of the business.

"Start with what customers see first."

A website, search result, message, photo, form, or follow-up can be the first point of trust. Improve the clearest gaps first.

"Use technology where it earns its place."

Design, content, search foundations, and practical systems should make the business easier to understand and easier to choose.

"Keep the work close."

Strategy, architecture, and creative direction stay connected to the work, with specialists brought in when a project needs them.

Capabilities

How projects come together.

Projects can combine web design, branding, content, local visibility, follow-up systems, and interactive work depending on what the business needs most.

The shape of the work stays practical: clarify the gap, build the right customer-facing pieces, and keep improving where it makes sense.
