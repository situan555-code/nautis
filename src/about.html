Our Story

Born from the full puzzle of modern business.

The foundation of Sunder was born out of a professional obsession with the full puzzle of modern business. Over the course of a career designing multi-channel digital campaigns, custom web systems, and large-scale technology deployments, a persistent pattern became clear: too many companies were held back by clunky, fragmented legacy systems that could not keep pace with modern customer expectations.

This sparked a deep fascination with the technology overhaul - the art of taking complex, outdated setups and rebuilding them into streamlined, high-performance engines. With the rapid emergence of AI and modern cloud tools, there has never been a better or more urgent time for businesses to modernize.

Real growth does not happen in silos. It happens when backend automation, striking design, physical environments, and B2B relationships operate as a single, unified system.

Driven by this passion to connect the dots and capitalize on this technological shift, Sunder was established to redesign the digital agency model, shifting the focus from isolated deliverables to complete ecosystem transformation.
