The Shift

Search is no longer only a list of links.

People still use Google, maps, and normal search results. But they are also asking longer questions inside AI-powered search tools, chat interfaces, browser assistants, and summaries that answer before a person ever reaches a website.

That changes the job of a website. It still needs to rank, load quickly, and convert. It also needs to explain the business clearly enough that machines can understand who the company is, what it does, where it works, and why its information should be trusted.

Traditional search

AI-powered discovery
