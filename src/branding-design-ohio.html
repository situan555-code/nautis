Branding

Branding and Design for Ohio Businesses

Sunder & Co. builds practical brand systems for Ohio businesses that need clearer visuals and a stronger first impression. The work can include logo refinement, identity systems, collateral, signage-ready assets, and website-aligned design direction.

Who this is for

Businesses whose visuals no longer match the work.

This is for local and regional businesses whose website, print materials, signage, social posts, proposals, or sales materials feel outdated or inconsistent.

What Sunder & Co. does

We clarify the visual system so customers see one recognizable business across web, print, content, and physical touchpoints. The goal is a brand that is easier to recognize, trust, and use in the real world.
