The 5-Pillar Maxing Framework (Technical Specs for 2026)

Pillar 1: Foundation

Verify ownership, lock primary + secondary categories, complete every attribute field, set accurate hours and service areas with geo-coordinates.

Pillar 2: Visual Proof

Upload 20–30+ recent, high-resolution photos of real work, team, and location. Add short videos and virtual tours.

Pillar 3: Services & Products

List every offering with detailed descriptions, pricing ranges where appropriate, and direct booking links.

Pillar 4: Engagement Engine

  • Post 1–2 times per week (case studies, offers, behind-the-scenes).
  • Proactively seed 15–20 expert Q&A pairs.
  • Respond to every review within 24 hours.

Pillar 5: Measurement & Iteration

Monitor the Performance tab weekly. Track action rate (target 5%+), discovery share, and photo/post engagement.
