Why Kiosks Convert in 2026

1. The "Endless Aisle" Strategy

Manufacturers often have thousands of SKU variations but can only show 5 physical models. A kiosk lets customers "Build" their perfect version in 3D in real-time, resulting in a 30% higher conversion rate for customized products.

2. The Data-Driven Sales Assistant

Kiosks don't get tired and don't forget to capture emails. By integrating directly with your CRM, you capture intent data a human might miss — leading to a 7.8x increase in qualified leads.

3. Reducing Friction in the Buy-Cycle

Industrial buyers are busy. A kiosk lets them scan a part, check specs, see inventory across warehouses, and place an order in under 60 seconds. This self-service model is preferred by 68% of B2B buyers under 45.

The ROI of Labor Savings

One of the fastest returns is reducing "low-value" labor. By letting kiosks handle basic inquiries, warranty registration, and simple orders, your high-value engineers can focus on complex consultative selling. One client saw a 28.6% reduction in floor labor costs within 6 months.

The Sunder Approach to 2026 Kiosk Deployment

  1. High-Fidelity 3D Content — Use the same 3D assets from your web configurator so customers can "touch" the digital product.
  2. Hardware/Software Unity — Custom enclosures that match your brand while optimized for touch and distance viewing.
  3. Real-Time Analytics — Track every tap. Know which products get attention and where customers drop off.
  4. Seamless Checkout — Let customers send a "Cart Link" to their phone via QR code to finish later.
