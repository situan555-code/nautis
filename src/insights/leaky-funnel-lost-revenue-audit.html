The 3 Most Common Funnel Leaks in 2026

1. The "Information Gap"

Modern B2B buyers want autonomy. If your website requires a "Request a Quote" form just to see basic technical specs or material availability, you're losing 40% of your leads to competitors who provide that data upfront. The Fix: Move your technical catalog and inventory levels to the "Front of Funnel" using a PIM system.

2. The "Hand-off" Black Hole

What happens when a lead is qualified? If it sits in an inbox for 24 hours while a sales manager "assigns" it, the lead's intent drops by 80%. The Fix: Implement automated, round-robin lead routing that puts a meeting on a salesperson's calendar in under 2 minutes.

3. The "Post-Quote" Silence

70% of B2B quotes are never followed up on more than once. In 2026, the sales cycle for complex equipment is 6-12 months. If you aren't providing value-added content (case studies, ROI models) during that period, you will be forgotten. The Fix: Trigger-based sales enablement sequences that keep your brand top-of-mind without manual effort.
