The 3 Pillars of a 2026 Stack Audit

1. Capability Overlap Analysis

Do you have a CRM with email marketing, a dedicated email tool, and a separate landing page builder that *also* does email? In 2026, the "Best of Breed" approach is being replaced by the "Best of Suite" approach to eliminate data silos. Consolidating these three tools into one platform like HubSpot or Salesforce can save $20k/year in subscription fees alone.

2. The "Integration Debt" Check

Tools that aren't integrated aren't helping you; they're creating work. We look for "manual bridges" — employees spending hours downloading CSVs from one tool to upload them into another. By automating these flows, we often reclaim 15–20 hours per week for marketing teams.

3. Usage & Adoption Scoring

If you're paying for a $2,000/month analytics suite but your team is still using a free Google sheet, you have an adoption problem. We look at the actual login data and feature usage. If a tool isn't being used, we cut it. No "just in case" subscriptions allowed.

The ROI of a "Clean" Stack

Beyond the direct cost savings, a consolidated stack performs better. When your email, ads, CRM, and website all share the same data in real-time, your personalization becomes 3x more effective.
